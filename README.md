# 🛒 Global Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive sales and profitability analysis of the Global Superstore dataset.
The goal is to uncover actionable business insights to support strategic decision making in sales, shipping and regional performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify sales trends across regions and time
* Analyze profit performance by category
* Evaluate shipping cost impact on profitability
* Detect top-performing sub-categories
* Highlight underperforming markets

---

## 🧰 Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel

  * Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
  * Pivot Tables
  * Charts & Dashboard Design

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

* **Total Sales:** $12.64M
* **Total Profit:** $1.47M
* **Profit Margin:** 11.61%
* **Total Shipping Cost:** $1.35M
* **No. of Sub-Categories:** 17

---

## 📌 Key Insights

* Technology generates the highest profit among all categories.
* APAC and EU markets incur the highest shipping costs.
* Some markets show high shipping cost but relatively low profit.
* Phones, Chairs and Copiers are top performing sub-categories.
* Sales show an upward trend towards year end.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Optimize shipping routes in high-cost markets.
* Invest more in Technology products.
* Promote high-performing sub-categories.
* Review logistics strategy in low-profit regions.
* Implement cost-control measures for freight.
